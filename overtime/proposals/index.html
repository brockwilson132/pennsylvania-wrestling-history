Design directions · for review

Rankings, cards and the team page

Everything below is drawn from the real archive. The header holds the only two global controls: class and time window. Each table gets one sort control. Pick a direction per card type (A, B or C) and the app gets rebuilt around it.

How it's scored

Wrestler rating is unchanged: his season strength from every bout at districts, regionals and states, on one scale where 1500 is an average state qualifier. A card shows his peak season.

School rating (per season) is the average rating of its lineup: its best wrestler at each weight that season, wherever he wrestled. A weight where the school entered no one counts as 1000 (a replacement-level wrestler). In seasons whose district bracket is not on file, an empty weight counts as 1067, the typical rating of a wrestler whose season ended at districts, so missing D3/D7 brackets don't drag those schools down.

District and region rating (per season) is how strong the top of that tournament was: the average rating of its four best wrestlers at each weight. That is a per-weight measure, so a big district only rates higher when its top four really are better.

Over a window, a unit's rating is the plain average of its season ratings inside the window; a school needs to have wrestled at least a third of those seasons to be listed. Score is how much they won: placement points (16-12-9-7-5-3-2-1) in the window, weighted by the page you're on, the same weights as the all-time team. On the State page only state results count; on a Region page, state 1.0 + regional 0.4; on District and School pages, state 1.0 + regional 0.4 + district 0.15. Score grows with size by design; Rating is how good they were and does not. Titles are individual state titles; team titles are the official PIAA team championships.

Power and Era are gone: Era (sum of state-point shares) is what Score plus Rating already say, and Power's recency weighting is replaced by choosing a time window.

Performance

Replaces the metric boxes and the level switch. Tap a season for its lineup.

Controls, simplified

  • Header: class (AAA / AA) and seasons (All-time, Last 10, Last 5, custom). Nothing else global.
  • One sort control per table: Rating (default) and Score; Titles added on district, region and state lists.
  • No level switch, no Medals/Power/Era sorts, no filter sheet on ranking pages (search covers schools and wrestlers).

Where brackets stay

  • Only for real tournaments: tap any district, regional or state result to open that bracket.
  • Ranking lists use the cards above, never the bracket drawing.

Data notes

  • District and region ratings start in 1974 for the two-class era; one-class seasons count toward a school's later class.
  • Team rating uses every bout on file; seasons before the 1970s have partial district brackets, so treat early numbers as approximate.
